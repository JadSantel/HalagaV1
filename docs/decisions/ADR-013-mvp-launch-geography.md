# ADR-013: Launch the MVP in Cagayan de Oro City

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-05

## Context

The marketplace needs enough relevant buyers near each seller to produce competing offers. Launching across a broad geography would increase nominal inventory while fragmenting demand and introducing shipping, distance, and regional operations before local marketplace behavior is validated.

The product's current examples and discovery context already point toward Cagayan de Oro City.

## Decision

The MVP launch market is Cagayan de Oro City, Philippines.

Supply acquisition, buyer acquisition, listing eligibility, location design, marketplace metrics, and operational support will be evaluated within this launch area.

## Consequences

- The MVP should optimize for local discovery and physical pickup or meetup within Cagayan de Oro City.
- Launch success must be assessed using local marketplace density rather than nationwide registration or listing totals.
- Listings need a city-level location and may need a more specific non-public area or barangay for useful matching.
- Exact home addresses must not be publicly exposed.
- Users or items outside the launch area require explicit handling, such as being ineligible, waitlisted, or visible without transaction support; that behavior remains unresolved.
- Expansion beyond Cagayan de Oro City requires evidence of local marketplace health and a separate rollout decision.
